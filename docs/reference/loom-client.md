# The Loom page and tests

What each part of the Loom page does, its tests, and the files shared with the server, in full. [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) keeps one line each.

## Play view (Preact + htm)

**Preact 10.29.8 + htm 3.1.1** — CDN via unpkg.com, no build (the Loom's play view: components in `window.Loom`, one store; `public/apps/loom/js/ui.js`, `shell.js` (the side panel, a bottom sheet on phones), the panel's content in `journal.js` (with the roll cards' dice to tap), the rules reference in `rules.js`, the trail of the maps above you, bottom left of the map, and peeking up it in `map.js` (L-904), people's tokens in `draw.js` `token()` (L-932: their picture clipped to a circle at its crop in a ring coloured by side, else their initials in it, standing upright on a small base in the grid view's depth order and fading walls in front of it, lying flat greyed and crossed when down, the one whose turn plays out ringed; pictures loaded once by link), the Character panel and the strip on the map in `character.js` (with experience toward the next level and a mark when one is ready, L-845), making a character for a new game in `creation.js`, the level-up form in the journal in `levelup.js` (L-845); the look's tokens in `loom.css`, each world's accent in `theme.js`) The player's token (L-933): `token-picker.js`, the picker the new-game flow's Token step (`creation.js`, the only step for a world with a premade character) and the Character panel's Token section (`character.js`) share: the world's player portraits and Upload your own (PNG, JPEG or WebP, at most 5 MB), the choice framed in its circle by dragging (or the arrow keys) and a zoom slider; a portrait goes to `loomCreateSave` or as a token turn, an upload through `loomUploadToken`; an uploaded token is read signed in (`state.js` `Loom.tokenUrl`, Storage's download endpoint with the ID token, kept as a blob URL) and drawn by `draw.js` from it. Tested in headless Chrome on a desktop and a phone (`tests/loom-token-picker.test.js`, `tests/fixtures/token-picker/`)

## Map engine (PixiJS)

**PixiJS 8.21.0** — CDN via unpkg.com (the Loom's map engine, `public/apps/loom/js/engine.js`: WebGL, canvas without it; the world map in `world.js`, towns in `town.js`, battle maps in `battle.js`, shared drawing in `draw.js`; every map's geometry drawn from its materials' and objects' art by one painter, `paint.js` (L-924: ground, height shading, lines, walls, doors and windows by state, objects, light; a map's own art in place of its ground and walls; kept per map in memory, built a few ms at a time; in three-quarter view since L-941: walls with faces at their height, doors and windows standing, an open door's leaf swung, objects with a standing picture (`materials.js` `standFor`, from the world's art set) on their feet at their size, all in one layer sorted by their feet with the drawer's tokens, which the grid view lays over its dark fog cut to the columns over squares seen, with room above the map; a click on a standing picture picks its object; since L-944 from the world's art set, its manifest fetched once, an atlas loaded once and drawn from by frames, anything the set lacks the built-in SVG; since L-943 contact shadows along walls' feet and under props and tokens standing, a floor fading over the one under it along its edge, a floor's variant picked by its id, and only what's on the screen drawn, `mapPaint.cull` as the camera moves; since L-942 the walls, doors and tall objects standing over a token, the pointer or the path preview fade to 30%, `mapPaint.fade`, which the grid view calls as they move), with `progress.js`'s progress effect while it draws)

## Tests (Jest)

**Jest 29** — Rules, Cloud Functions, Loom, Cartographer and MCP tests against the Firestore and Storage emulators; headless Chrome through Playwright (a test dependency, its Chromium installed in CI) for the map painter (`tests/loom-paint.test.js`, with a screenshot kept in `tests/fixtures/paint/`; `UPDATE_PAINT=1` makes it again) and an object's click menu and card (`tests/loom-objects-menu.test.js`; PixiJS, Preact and htm served from the tests' own copy at the page's versions by `tests/helpers/fixture-server.js`) and the art swatches, and tokens standing (`tests/loom-tokens-draw.test.js`, L-932)

## Shared with the server

**Shared with the server** — a rule both the Cloud Functions and the Loom page run (`town-paths.js`, its paths in town; `map-geometry.js`, a map's geometry, L-921; `materials.js`, the built-in materials and object catalog, L-925, and art sets read from their manifests, L-944, each set's `public/apps/loom/art/sets/<set>/manifest.json` copied as `functions/loom-canon/art-sets/<set>.json`; `map-paths.js`, moving and distances in feet, L-922, which replaced `grid-paths.js`) lives in `functions/loom-canon/` and `public/apps/loom/js/` with identical bytes, since `functions/` deploys on its own. A test fails if the copies differ: edit both.
