# The Loom page and tests

What each part of the Loom page does, its tests, and the files shared with the server, in full. [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) keeps one line each.

## Play view (Preact + htm)

**Preact 10.29.8 + htm 3.1.1** — CDN via unpkg.com, no build (the Loom's play view: components in `window.Loom`, one store; `public/apps/loom/js/ui.js`, `shell.js` (the side panel, a bottom sheet on phones), the panel's content in `journal.js` (with the roll cards' dice to tap), the rules reference in `rules.js`, the trail of the maps above you, bottom left of the map, and peeking up it in `map.js` (L-904), people's tokens in `draw.js` `token()` (L-932: their picture clipped to a circle at its crop in a ring coloured by side, else their initials in it, standing upright on a small base in the grid view's depth order and fading walls in front of it, lying flat greyed and crossed when down, the one whose turn plays out ringed; pictures loaded once by link), the Character panel and the strip on the map in `character.js` (with experience toward the next level and a mark when one is ready, L-845), making a character for a new game in `creation.js`, the level-up form in the journal in `levelup.js` (L-845); the look's tokens in `loom.css`, each world's accent in `theme.js`) The player's token (L-933): `token-picker.js`, the picker the new-game flow's Token step (`creation.js`, the only step for a world with a premade character) and the Character panel's Token section (`character.js`) share: the world's player portraits and Upload your own (PNG, JPEG or WebP, at most 5 MB), the choice framed in its circle by dragging (or the arrow keys) and a zoom slider; a portrait goes to `loomCreateSave` or as a token turn, an upload through `loomUploadToken`; an uploaded token is read signed in (`state.js` `Loom.tokenUrl`, Storage's download endpoint with the ID token, kept as a blob URL) and drawn by `draw.js` from it. Tested in headless Chrome on a desktop and a phone (`tests/loom-token-picker.test.js`, `tests/fixtures/token-picker/`)

## Map engine (PixiJS)

**PixiJS 8.21.0** — CDN via unpkg.com (the Loom's map engine, `public/apps/loom/js/engine.js`: WebGL, canvas without it; the world map in `world.js`, towns in `town.js`, battle maps in `battle.js`, shared drawing in `draw.js`; every map's geometry drawn from its materials' and objects' art by one painter, `paint.js` (L-924: ground, height shading, lines, walls, doors and windows by state, objects, light; a map's own art in place of its ground and walls; kept per map in memory, built a few ms at a time; in three-quarter view since L-941: walls with faces at their height, doors and windows standing, an open door's leaf swung, objects with a standing picture (`materials.js` `standFor`, from the world's art set) on their feet at their size, all in one layer sorted by their feet with the drawer's tokens, which the grid view lays over its dark fog cut to the columns over squares seen, with room above the map; a click on a standing picture picks its object; since L-944 from the world's art set, its manifest fetched once, an atlas loaded once and drawn from by frames, anything the set lacks the built-in SVG; since L-943 contact shadows along walls' feet and under props and tokens standing, a floor fading over the one under it along its edge, a floor's variant picked by its id, and only what's on the screen drawn, `mapPaint.cull` as the camera moves; since L-942 the walls, doors and tall objects standing over a token, the pointer or the path preview fade to 30%, `mapPaint.fade`, which the grid view calls as they move), with `progress.js`'s progress effect while it draws; since L-714 the world map of a region map with no image also draws who holds the land, `world.js`: each realm's edge stroked in six fading bands in its colour and clipped to its land, so it shows on the inside only, with its name at its centre, under the routes and places; built once per change and kept in map space, out-of-view realms hidden; nothing with an image) **Across country** (L-367; `wild-view.js`, `world.js`): where loomGetMap sends `wilds`, a click on open ground or a place's "Travel across country" menu item previews the way, planned in the page by `wild-paths.js` on the same inputs as the turn (`Loom.wildView.planTo`), drawn as a dashed line with the distance, time, ground crossed and a wildness rating on the card (calm, wild, dangerous or deadly by the average danger, under 0.8, 1.3 and 1.8); setting out sends `travel` with `offRoad` (`Loom.play.travelAcross`). A journey under way is drawn as its way ahead, a tent at each camp, a ring where it heads and the save's marker in the wilds, with Continue and Stop on the card (`Loom.play.journeyOn`, `journeyStop`). `Loom.world.previewed()` and `trekDrawn()` are for tests.

**The isometric view** (L-951; [`isometric-view.md`](../../planning/isometric-view.md) §4.1, IS1, IS2, IN2, C2): grid maps (`battle.js`) drawn in 2:1 isometric, a 5 ft square a diamond twice as wide as tall, the map's north to the top right, a foot of height rising 1.22 floor units up the screen. Behind a switch until L-953 makes it the default: `?view=iso` on the page, or `Loom.battle.setIso(true)` (`Loom.battle.iso()` says which). What lies on the floor (the painter's ground, the reach, areas, hazards, fog, the path and selections) is drawn on the flat grid as before, in three containers (`floor`, `floorDim`, `floorOver`) laid on the scene by one transform; tokens, names and foes' cards are placed on the isometric screen, upright; the standing layer's corner is at the map's north-west corner; clicks and hover are worked back to the floor. The camera frames the map's diamond with room above for the tallest wall at the true rise; the floor is culled to what the screen shows of it; the minimap draws the diamond (`rect.shape`, `overlay.js`); the faint 5 ft diamonds show only while a move or an aim is being chosen. Town and region maps are unchanged. `Loom.battle.mapPoint` returns a point in feet on the scene (isometric with the switch), `floorPoint` the reverse, and `drawn()` your token, the names, the cards and whether the grid shows, for tests (`tests/loom-iso-view.test.js`).

**The painter in isometric** (L-952; [`isometric-view.md`](../../planning/isometric-view.md) §4.2, IS3, IS5, AR1, AR2): with `paint.js`'s option `iso: { rise }`, the floor is drawn as before for the drawer to project, and the standing layer is built on the isometric screen (`iso.js`, pure, `Loom.iso`: a floor point (x, y) at a height h at (x − y, (x + y) / 2 − h)). Walls are prisms, the line given a thickness by material (`ISO_THICK`, about a foot), their face toward the camera and their end there with the material's face picture laid on by an affine map, shaded by the way they face (lit from the left), their top's picture the cap; a wall seen edge-on (running north-west) shows only its cap and end. They're cut into pieces of at most a tile and a half (the design said half a tile; see its §4.2: ordered by footprint, longer pieces still order every pair right, and half a tile drew 1.3–1.5× slower than three-quarter). Doors stand in walls both ways, a shut one a panel its front fills (a locked one tinted), an open one a leaf swung on its hinge, the wall over the doorway; windows the same. Props stand on their footprint by facing (`iso.facing`: front south, back mirrored west, back north, front mirrored east; a picture painted long one way mirrored to lie the other), from the set's isometric pictures (`materials.js isoFor`, a manifest entry's `iso: { facings: [front, back], foot?, long? }`, checked by `checkManifest`; L-954 paints them) else their three-quarter one, else flat as before; their contact shadows on the floor plane. A map's own painting never stands in for its walls or doors on a grid (AR2): they stand from its data over it. What stands is drawn back to front by footprint (`iso.order`: two footprints apart are ordered by the line between them, overlapping ones by depth), made once a map is built (`stats().orderMs`); the drawer's tokens are placed in that order as they move (`Loom.mapPaint.place(key, node, { print, box, depth })`, `iso.slot`; where none fits, the order is made again with them in). Culled on the isometric screen (`view(key, rect, stand)`, the grid view's `cullFloor`); dimmed by the fog where out of sight (`Loom.mapPaint.shade`, the grid view's `standShade`, its seen-before dimming then lying under what stands); `outlineOf(wrap)` a piece's outline on the screen for picking (L-953). `describe()` says what of a slab `shows` (`face`, `end`, `cap`), a prop's `facing` and `mirror`, and what's `dimmed`. Tests: `tests/loom-iso-order.test.js` (pure: the depth order's cases, whole maps, facings) and `tests/loom-iso-paint.test.js` (headless: a forest track's screenshot, faces both ways, props by facing, the order in the grid view, fog, culling, and the large fixture within 1.2× of the three-quarter view's time).

## Tests (Jest)

**Jest 29** — Rules, Cloud Functions, Loom, Cartographer and MCP tests against the Firestore and Storage emulators; headless Chrome through Playwright (a test dependency, its Chromium installed in CI) for the map painter (`tests/loom-paint.test.js`, with a screenshot kept in `tests/fixtures/paint/`; `UPDATE_PAINT=1` makes it again) and an object's click menu and card (`tests/loom-objects-menu.test.js`; PixiJS, Preact and htm served from the tests' own copy at the page's versions by `tests/helpers/fixture-server.js`) and the art swatches, and tokens standing (`tests/loom-tokens-draw.test.js`, L-932) The map across country (L-367) is `tests/loom-wild-map-draw.test.js` (headless, on the real `mapView`: the preview against the server's plan, the menus, the journey drawn again after a reload, off) and `tests/loom-wild-map-play.test.js` (what loomGetMap sends and the camps kept leg by leg).

## Shared with the server

**Shared with the server** — a rule both the Cloud Functions and the Loom page run (`town-paths.js`, its paths in town; `map-geometry.js`, a map's geometry, L-921; `materials.js`, the built-in materials and object catalog, L-925, and art sets read from their manifests, L-944, each set's `public/apps/loom/art/sets/<set>/manifest.json` copied as `functions/loom-canon/art-sets/<set>.json`; `map-paths.js`, moving and distances in feet, L-922, which replaced `grid-paths.js`; `wild-paths.js`, the quickest way across a region map's painted ground on a coarse grid, routes as lanes, L-361, loaded as `Loom.wildPaths` for the journey preview) lives in `functions/loom-canon/` and `public/apps/loom/js/` with identical bytes, since `functions/` deploys on its own. A test fails if the copies differ: edit both.
