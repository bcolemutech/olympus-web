The library of art for worlds

You don't have access to the Image Studio. It comes with the Cartographer: ask an admin to grant it in The Pantheon.

Make an image

A Gemini image model draws it, and it goes into the library with its prompt. Every image is charged: a draft costs about $0.07, a final image about $0.13. Several can be made at once; they appear below as they finish.
